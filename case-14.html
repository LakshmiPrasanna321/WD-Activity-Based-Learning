Nutritious Recipes

Protein Oats Bowl

Calories: 320 kcal | Protein: 15g | Carbs: 45g | Fat: 8g

Ingredient List:

  • Rolled Oats - 50g
  • Low Fat Milk - 200ml
  • Banana - 1 medium
  • Chia Seeds - 1 tsp
  • Honey - 1 tsp

Recipe:

Cook oats in milk, add banana slices, chia seeds and honey. Serve hot.

Grilled Chicken Salad

Calories: 280 kcal | Protein: 30g | Carbs: 10g | Fat: 12g

Ingredient List:

  • Grilled Chicken - 150g
  • Lettuce, Tomato, Cucumber
  • Olive Oil - 1 tsp
  • Lemon Juice - 1 tbsp

Recipe:

Grill chicken, chop veggies, mix with olive oil and lemon. Healthy lunch ready.

Green Detox Smoothie

Calories: 180 kcal | Protein: 5g | Carbs: 28g | Fat: 3g

Ingredient List:

  • Spinach - 1 cup
  • Apple - 1
  • Cucumber - 1/2
  • Water / Coconut Water - 200ml

Recipe:

Blend all ingredients in mixer until smooth. Best for morning detox.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Drink 3-4 litres of water daily
  • Eat 5 small meals instead of 3 large meals
  • Avoid junk food, oily food and excess sugar
  • Include fruits, vegetables and protein in every meal
  • Do not skip breakfast - it is the most important meal
  • Sleep 7-8 hours daily for better metabolism
